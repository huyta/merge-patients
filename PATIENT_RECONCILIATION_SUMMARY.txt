3. NORMALIZATION METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To ensure accurate clinical record linkage across hospital datasets without false
negatives or erroneous matches, the following normalization pipeline was applied:
  1. Whitespace Trimming    : Stripped leading/trailing spaces, tabs, and carriage returns.
  2. Quote Stripping        : Removed outer single/double quotes around fields.
  3. Delimiter Removal      : Removed all formatting hyphens ('-') and spaces (' ').
  4. Case Normalization     : Uppercased all alphanumeric characters (e.g., passport/old IC).
  5. Leading Zero Retention : Strict string handling; leading zeros are preserved (e.g. '010203...').
  6. Missing Token Filtering: Placeholder strings ('NA', 'N/A', 'NIL', 'NULL', 'UNKNOWN',
                              '-', '--', 'TIADA', 'DUMMY') are treated strictly as missing
                              NRIC values and excluded from matching.
  7. Independent Matching   : Comparison is performed strictly on normalized NRIC.
                              Patient names, DOB, or other attributes are never used to infer matches.

8. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR HKL & IPR CLINICAL & IT TEAMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIORITIZE IPR-ONLY PATIENTS FOR SYNCHRONIZATION:
   The 2,652 patients existing only at IPR represent the primary target for initial
   inbound backfill into HKL, as IPR frequently dispatches respiratory lab specimens
   to HKL for specialized pathology analysis.

2. CLEAN UP TEST ACCOUNTS PRIOR TO AUTOMATED SYNC GO-LIVE:
   Accounts with NRICs 'TEST', 'TEST00', 'TEST000', 'TEST11', '007', '008', '1000'
   should be marked IsDeleted=1 in both local databases before enabling bidirectional sync
   to avoid propagating non-clinical test data.

3. RESOLVE DUPLICATE ENTRIES AT IPR:
   The 12 duplicate NRIC instances at IPR should be consolidated. The active record with
   complete demographic details (Name, DOB) should be retained, and the blank duplicate
   marked IsDeleted=1.

4. DATABASE INPUT NORMALIZATION TRIGGER:
   Implement an application-level or database-level trigger at patient registration to strip
   hyphens and leading/trailing whitespace automatically, preventing future duplicate entries.
